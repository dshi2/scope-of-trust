# Claim-evidence rubric for expert review

Generated by `generate.py`. For every claim that a condition's gold marks as established, this lists the claim, what the record checked and found, the builder's reading of quantifier, population and period, and the residual risk. **Builder rationale is not validation.** A welfare scientist or certification auditor fills the last two columns. A disputed row means that gold is not approved, and model answers on it are not scored as errors.
