**Batch declaration.** Batch bc is one of three separately authored batches (ba, bb, bc) of the PI-approved expansion from 12 to 36 development cases. It was authored after the base and extension stimuli were frozen (`stimuli_dev.jsonl`, sha256 `368e1d0e…8e67`; `stimuli_ext.jsonl`, sha256 `c9d92a01…63d0`) and is run as a separately declared batch (`stimuli_bc.jsonl`) with the same frozen model configuration (`configs/p2_dev_eval_v1.json`), renderer, system prompt, task text and condition matrix. Batch cases were not selected or edited based on any model output. The reporting rule for the expansion is the one declared with the batch run; this file does not restate or change it.

## What batch bc adds

- **Populations beyond farms:** laboratory mice and zebrafish (breeding suppliers), stray dogs in contracted kennels, cats and rabbits held for rehoming, working sled dogs at a tour operator, zoo marmosets and tamarins, trekking donkeys. Each operator supplies animals or an animal-based service under contract, so the frozen "procurement committee / supplier" wording applies unchanged.
- **Practice types:** nesting material, water quality, kennel enrichment, hiding places, workload limit, enclosure space, foraging enrichment, rider and pack-load limit.
- **Outcome evidence sources:** coat scoring by a technician from outside the breeding team, daily health-check removals against a tank census, day-7 scoring by a contracted behaviourist, veterinary nurse weighing records, a contracted vet's clinical records, weekly checks by a duty animal care officer, quarterly alopecia scoring by a veterinary team, quarterly yard visits by a regional animal health officer.
- **Indicators:** barbering, illness removals, kennel repetitive behaviour, weight loss after admission, lameness/strain treatments, bar-biting or repetitive digging, alopecia, saddle or girth sores.
- **Issuers:** eight new fictional issuers (none shared with the base or extension sets).

## Not covered (known gaps, unchanged by this batch)

- **Template diversity: one family.** Batch cases use the same audit-record layout (`kv-record-v1`) and dossier layout as the base cases, by design (same renderer). Format-specific behaviour still cannot be separated from the effect.
- **Period and population scope.** No condition varies the audited period or audits a subset of the population.
- **Independence.** All cases come from one builder pipeline. Analysis must cluster on case.
- **Realism.** Thresholds, indicators and methods are unreviewed placeholders (spec §8.6); `EXPERT_VERDICTS.csv` has no rows for batch-bc cases yet (human-owned file, not written by this script).
