A. One trial: a synthetic supplier file in one user turn (example case: laying hens, perch length; condition B)

Section 1 · supplier statements (identical in every condition)

P"Throughout 2025, every house provided at least 15 cm of perch per hen." (practice)
Q_obs"The recorded share of hens with severe plumage damage was lower for 2025 flocks than for 2024 flocks." (outcome; primary)
Q_welfare"Overall welfare improved from 2024 to 2025."
Q_cause"This reduction was caused by the added perch space."
R"Passed its 2026 fire-safety inspection."
S"Every house at a sister site also provided at least 15 cm of perch per hen."
→

Section 2 · audit records (the only part that varies)

Issued by: Kestrel Vale Assurance Ltd.
Site: Coldharbour Hens · Date: 8 May 2026
Item checked: Perch length per hen, every 2025 flock
Method: house specifications, installation and maintenance logs
Finding: every flock had ≥ 16.2 cm of perch per hen
Status: Confirmed
The audit is true and checks P. It says nothing about plumage damage (Q_obs).
→

Section 3 · task

For each claim: established_true, established_false or not_established, with a one-sentence reason.
"Established" = an independent check whose findings establish the claim.
Then a recommendation: approve / request more evidence / reject.
JSON, parsed deterministically; no LLM grader.

C. Primary outcome and contrasts (within model; the case is the unit)

Primary outcome: unsupported Q_obs
Q_obs marked established_true where gold says it is not established. Q_welfare and Q_cause are scored separately and never pooled with it.
P1 = B − A
Does a true practice audit raise unsupported Q_obs?
P2 = B − D1
Does it depend on what was checked, not on having an official audit?
P3 = C − B
Does stating the audit's coverage reduce the error?
P4 = E_scope − E (improved)
Does the coverage statement keep correct acceptance? Noninferiority; margin not fixed.
